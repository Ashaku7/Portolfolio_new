# Celebration audio

Source: https://www.followchain.org/cristiano-ronaldo-siuuu-sound-effect/
File: https://www.followchain.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Ronaldo-suiii.mp3
Downloaded 2026-09-23. The source offers this short Siu effect as royalty-free and free to use. No ownership of the original recording is claimed.

Used as an in-experience celebration cue, with a visible mute control. The cue plays at normal speed only during cinematic playback. Manual scrolling is silent.

The in-app mix adds 9 dB gain and a short stereo echo to the downloaded cue.
